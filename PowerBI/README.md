# Adidas US Sales Analysis 📊

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Adidas retail sales in the United States using **Power BI**.

The dataset covers **2020 and 2021** and contains information about retailers, invoice dates, regions, products, pricing, units sold, sales, operating profit, and operating margin. 

The goal is to understand sales and profitability patterns, identify regional performance, and recognize top-performing products and retailers. 

## Objectives

- Understand overall sales dynamics.
- Analyze key business KPIs.
- Identify seasonal sales patterns.
- Evaluate geographical and regional performance.
- Analyze product and retailer performance. 

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard focuses on:

- **Total Sales**
- **Operating Profit**
- **Total Units Sold**
- **Average Price per Unit**
- **Average Operating Margin**

| Analysis | Visualization |

These visuals cover sales trends, geographical performance, and product and retailer contributions. 

## Key Analysis Areas

### Sales & Seasonal Trends
Monthly sales are analyzed to identify changes in sales performance over time.

### Geographical Performance
Sales are examined at state and regional levels to identify stronger and weaker markets.

### Product Performance
Product-level sales are compared to identify the products contributing most to overall sales.

### Retailer Performance
Retailer-level sales are analyzed to recognize top-performing retail partners.

### Profitability
Operating profit and operating margin are included to evaluate business profitability alongside sales.

## Tools Used

- **Power BI**
- **Power Query** for data preparation and transformation
