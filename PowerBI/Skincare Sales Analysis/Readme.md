 # 🌿 Skincare Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Power BI dashboard created to analyze skincare sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, returns, and customer satisfaction.

The report is divided into three interactive dashboard pages:

### Sales Overview
Customer & Product Insights
Returns & Customer Satisfaction

## 🎯 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following business metrics:

1.**Total Sales: $1.18M**

2.**Total Orders: 1K**

3.**Total Customers: 500**

4.**Average Order Value: $940.28**

5.**Total Quantity Sold: 3K**

6.**Total Returns: 79**

7.**Return Rate: 6.32%**

8.**Average Customer Rating: 3.91**

9.**Delivered Order Percentage: 82%**


### 📊 Dashboard Pages

1. **Sales Overview**

 This page provides an overview of overall sales performance through:

- Total sales and order KPIs
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales by product category
- Sales by sales channel
- Orders by order status

Filters: Order Date, Category, and Sales Channel.

2. **Customer & Product Insights**

This page focuses on customer behavior and product performance.

- Sales by state
 
- Customers by age group

- Customers by acquisition channel

- Top products by sales

Filters: Gender, State, and Category.

3. **Returns & Customer Satisfaction**

This page analyzes product returns and customer feedback.

- Returns by return reason
- Returns by product category
- Average rating by category
- Returns by refund status
- Customer rating distribution

Filters: Category and Return Reason.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Serum is the highest-performing product category by sales.
- Maharashtra generates the highest sales among the states.
- The 25–34 age group represents the largest customer segment.
- Google Search is the leading customer acquisition channel.
- Skin irritation is the most common reason for product returns.
- Moisturizer has the highest average customer rating.
- Most customer reviews are concentrated around ratings 4 and 5.
- The overall return rate is 6.32%, while 82% of orders were delivered successfully.
